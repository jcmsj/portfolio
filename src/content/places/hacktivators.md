**Hacktivators** — a group of students and recent graduates leveling up in
software development by joining competitions and hackathons. This crew appears
throughout the city: the HackforGov monument, the ICP beacon, half of
Innovation Park.

Also home to our **ACM** and **JPCS** student chapters at TIP QC — hubs of
activity for the computing community at large.
